  • Non-exclusive (beat may be licensed to others)
  • WAV file (24-bit, untagged) + trackouts (stems) optional
  • Up to 100,000 streams across all platforms
  • Up to 10,000 audio downloads/sales

🥇 Exclusive License — $300-$1,000+

  • Exclusive ownership — the beat is removed from sale, no other artist will license it
  • WAV file (24-bit) + full trackouts (stems)
  • Unlimited streams, sales, and performances

  • Must credit "Prod. UniBeatz" in song title or description
  • Producer retains the master recording rights and 50% publishing of the composition unless additional buyout fee is negotiated

Master Buyout: For full ownership including master rights and 100% publishing, additional fees apply (typically 2-3x exclusive license price). Contact us for custom buyout negotiations.

📄 Beat License Agreement

By purchasing a beat license from UniBeatz Production, you (the "Licensee") agree to the following terms with UniBeatz Production (the "Licensor"):

1. Grant of License

Upon receipt of full payment, Licensor grants Licensee the non-transferable, limited rights specified in the purchased license tier (Basic, Premium, or Exclusive) to use the licensed beat ("Composition") for the creation of one or more derivative musical works ("Songs").

2. Producer Credit Required

Licensee must include the credit "Prod. UniBeatz" or "Produced by UniBeatz" in:

  • The song title (e.g., "Song Name (Prod. UniBeatz)") OR
  • The song description on streaming platforms
  • Music video descriptions
  • Any commercial release metadata

3. Restrictions

Licensee may NOT:

  • Resell, license, or sublicense the unaltered Composition
  • Register the Composition for copyright in their own name
  • Use the Composition in any sample pack, beat marketplace, or instrumental compilation
  • Claim authorship of the Composition
  • Use the Composition for material that promotes hate speech, violence, or discrimination

4. Royalties

For Basic and Premium licenses, Licensor retains 50% publishing (writer's share) of the Composition. Mechanical, performance, and sync royalties shall be split accordingly through standard PRO (BMI/ASCAP/SESAC) registration.

For Exclusive licenses, publishing splits are 50/50 unless renegotiated as part of a master buyout.

5. Term & Termination

Basic and Premium licenses are perpetual but subject to the usage limits stated. Once Licensee exceeds the streaming/sales limits, they must upgrade their license. Licensor may terminate this license for material breach (e.g., failure to credit, resale of beat) with 30 days notice.

6. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the United States. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in the producer's home state.
